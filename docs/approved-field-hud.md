# Approved Veyrak field HUD

The field HUD now follows the approved visual direction: compact Council portrait and HP/Core bars, circular objective minimap, edge-mounted inventory/character controls, icon combat controls and one continuous eight-slot hotbar.

The hotbar accepts weapons, utilities and food. Existing three-food-slot saves migrate into the new eight-slot format. The active slot is saved. Weapons can be readied from the hotbar, consumables activate directly, and utilities provide contextual feedback in the training area.

Portrait and landscape use separate placement rules rather than shrinking a desktop layout. Dialogue temporarily clears the field controls instead of layering instructional text over the world.

Full alternate-weapon animation sheets, equipment-specific world sprites and actual scanner/repair/harvest world interactions remain later RPG work.
